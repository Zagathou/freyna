FREYNA Leofwin

Freyna has gained incredible power through the Ultimate Project. She utilizes the venomous substances produced by her body to trap enemies in swamps of toxins. Affiliation: Colon Special Forces, Descendant Corps!

Attributes

Toxic / Tech / Dimension

Skills

Q — Venom Trauma (Tech): Fires a Toxic Bullet, dealing damage to hit enemies. The Toxic Bullet explodes, creating a Toxic Puddle and inflicting Room 0 Trauma on those it hits. Room 0 Trauma deals continuous damage and applies Panic to nearby enemies.
C — Defense Mechanism (Dimension): Gains Plague Bodyarmor. Inflicts Room 0 Trauma on the attacking enemy. Room 0 Trauma deals continuous damage and applies Despair to nearby enemies. (gets interchanged by the Module: Toxic Stimulation)
V — Putrid Venom (Tech): Throw poison to create a Toxic Swamp. Enemies that touch the Toxic Swamp are inflicted with Room 0 Trauma and Venom-Soaked. Venom-Soaked enemies leave Toxic Footprints as they move. Toxic Footprints apply Room 0 Trauma to enemies on contact. Room 0 Trauma deals continuous damage and applies Decay to nearby enemies.
Z — Venom Baptism (Tech): Equips a Unique Weapon. Unequipped after all bullets are consumed or after a certain period of time has passed. Enemies hit by the Unique Weapon bullets are inflicted with Room 0 Trauma. Room 0 Trauma deals continuous damage and applies Nightmare to nearby enemies.

CONTAGION Links (Passive Skill): When an enemy inflicted with Room 0 Trauma is killed, it spawns a Toxic Puddle. Enemies that step in the Toxic Puddle are inflicted with Room 0 Trauma. Room 0 Trauma deals continuous damage and applies Toxic Reaction to nearby enemies.

Transcendent Skill Modules

(17 Rutile) Contagion: When an enemy inflicted with Room 0 Trauma is killed, a contagion of Room 0 Trauma surrounds it.
(16 Xantic) Neurotoxin Synthesis: Modifies the effects of Room 0 Trauma. Does not spread Poison, but inflicts ATK reduction and Weaken Regeneration.
(16 Xantic) Toxic Mixture: Modifies the Room 0 Trauma effect to Necrosis. Firearm ATK increases with the number of Necrosis stacks.
(15 Cerulean) Toxic Stimulation: Defense Mechanism skill conversion. Grants Plague Bodyarmor to nearby allies. 
(14 Rutile) Venom Synthesis: Putrid Venom skill conversion. Leaves behind a Toxic Footprint after every step. Inflicts Room 0 Trauma on enemies coming in contact with Toxic Footprint.
(14 Rutile) Venom Injection: When using skill, continuously consumes resources to grant self Toxic Sense. While Toxic Sense is active, hitting an enemy with a firearm or a Skill inflicts Corrosion. (Most powerful Module, but... it consumes HP very fast! Increase HP, Shield + DEF)
